Automatic Hand Sanitizer Dispenser (AHSD)

By Ronen Acharya

Automatic Hand Sanitizer Dispenser (AHSD) dispenses a regulated amount of hand sanitizer with help of an Ultrasonic sensor (HC-SR04) and Arduino UNO. This device will help avoid contact on the sanitizer flask in public places and hospitals. The major benefit of this device is the avoidance of unnecessary contact on the sanitizer bottle. In addition, it also ensures efficient use of the sanitizer as it regulates the amount it dispenses. This is also economical and can be fabricated within a cost of NRS 4000-5000 per piece only.

Materials

  • Arduino Uno
  • Ultrasonic sensor
  • Motor Shield (L298N)
  • 6V water pump motor
  • Wires
  • 12V adaptor
  • Others including frame material and decorating items
